About

Everything affecting your day in Neepawa, on one page.

CityPulse pulls live data from official public feeds — weather, severe-weather alerts, highway conditions, closures, and cameras — and refreshes itself around the clock. There is no human in the loop.

What updates automatically

  • Current weather, next 12 hours & 3-day forecast — Open-Meteo, for Neepawa's exact coordinates. Fetched live while the page is open; if that fails, the page shows the last automatic update and says so.
  • Severe weather alerts — Environment Canada (Brandon forecast region, the closest coverage to Neepawa). Rechecked roughly every 1–2 hours; official alerts appear as a red banner.
  • Road closures & traffic events — Manitoba 511, filtered to within 60 km of Neepawa. Rechecked roughly every 1–2 hours.
  • Highway driving conditions — Manitoba 511 winter road condition reports for highways around Neepawa (PTH 16, PTH 5, Hwy 1 and area). Rechecked roughly every 1–2 hours; mainly published in winter.
  • Highway cameras — the nearest Manitoba 511 cameras, with images that refresh while you have the page open.
  • Air quality & wildfire smoke — Open-Meteo air-quality model for Neepawa's coordinates, shown as a Canadian-style AQHI value. Fetched live while the page is open.
  • Active wildfires — satellite fire detections from Natural Resources Canada (CWFIS), clustered into fire zones within 200 km and plotted on the map.
  • Whitemud River level — the Water Survey of Canada gauge nearest Neepawa (Whitemud River near Keyes, station 05LL005), with a 6-hour rise/fall trend as an early flood signal. Rechecked roughly every 1–2 hours.

Data caveats

  • Environment Canada alerts come from the Brandon forecast region — Neepawa shares almost all severe-weather alerts with it, but very localized warnings may not appear.
  • Weather values are model data interpolated to Neepawa's coordinates; the nearest official observation station is Brandon Airport.
  • Road conditions are seasonal — Manitoba 511 reports them mainly from fall to spring.
  • The AQHI shown is computed from the latest modelled concentrations (the official index uses 3-hour station averages) — treat it as a close estimate.
  • Wildfire zones are satellite heat detections, not official incident reports; small or new fires can be missed, and rare false positives (e.g. industrial heat) can appear.
  • River trend is measured at the Keyes gauge ~26 km downstream of Neepawa; town-level flooding can differ. For official flood forecasts see the Manitoba flood information.

How to read freshness

  • Live — weather and air quality fetched by your browser from Open-Meteo when the page loads (and every minute while it stays open).
  • Updated X ago — alerts, road information, cameras, wildfires and the river gauge, from the automatic update. Hover or long-press a time to see the exact date and time.
  • Pipeline looks stalled (amber) — the automatic update is more than 4 hours old. Check the official sources before travelling.
  • Unconfirmed / No data (grey) — a source didn’t respond. CityPulse never treats a missing source as “all clear”.

Using the dashboard

  • Map — locked by default so scrolling the page never moves it. Tap a marker for details any time; press Enable map interaction to pan and zoom, and Lock map (or Esc) to stop.
  • AJ — the small sphere in the corner is CityPulse’s guide. Ask it where things are, or what the loaded data says about weather, alerts, roads, hazards, cameras or data age. AJ runs entirely in your browser, answers only from data this page has loaded, and says so when something is unavailable or out of date. It isn’t a general chatbot and doesn’t forecast anything itself.
  • Appearance — the half-filled circle in the header switches between Light (the default), Dark and Match device.

Privacy

No analytics, advertising or tracking. Your appearance choice is stored only in your own browser. The AJ assistant runs entirely in your browser: questions you type are never sent anywhere. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap, camera images from Manitoba 511, and fonts from Google Fonts, so those services see standard web requests from your browser.

What this is not

⚠ Not an emergency service

If you see downed power lines, a fire, a gas leak, or anyone hurt — call 911. CityPulse is informational only and should never delay an emergency call.

How it's built

Static HTML/CSS/JS hosted on GitHub Pages. A scheduled GitHub Actions workflow polls the public feeds and republishes the site with fresh data (typically every 1–2 hours; GitHub drops most scheduled firings), and current weather is additionally fetched live in your browser straight from Open-Meteo. There is no server and no database — which also means there is nothing to hack, nothing to overload, and nothing that costs money to run. The Manitoba 511 API key lives in an encrypted GitHub secret and never reaches the browser.
